## Project Name: 

FitLog — Workout & Fitness Tracker

## Short description:

 FitLog is a modern, responsive web application built to help users explore exercises, track daily fitness plans, and manage saved workouts effortlessly. Featuring an intuitive dark-themed user interface, FitLog makes logging workouts seamless and motivating.

---

## 5 key Features

-  **Workout Library & Details:** Browse a variety of exercises complete with target muscle groups, required equipment, difficulty levels, sets, reps, and step-by-step instructions.
-  **Interactive "Today's Plan":** Easily add exercises to your daily workout plan, track total time/calories burned, and mark completed routines.
-  **Save for Later:** Bookmark your favorite workouts to access them anytime in your saved list.
-  **Dynamic Fitness Dashboard:** Automatically calculates real-time workout stats including total exercises, estimated duration (minutes), and calorie output.
-  **Smart Sorting & Filtering:** Organize your workout lists by duration, calorie burn, or name to suit your daily training goals.
